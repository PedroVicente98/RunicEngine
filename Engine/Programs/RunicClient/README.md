# RunicClient

The Engine's own game-agnostic client: the Engine's client layers with the GodotLayer on top, with neutral `engine:` test content. Engine slices show their client results here (debug drawing, moving entities, the capsule on terrain); RunicGame's GameClient is the same composition plus game modules. Never shipped.

- **Status:** Has code since GDB-1 (slice 1): the GodotLayer on top of an input-trace layer, on the neutral test project in `Project/` (one Button); `--input-check` pushes scripted clicks and a key (the CTests `godot.runic_client_headless` and `godot.runic_client_input`).
- **Slice 2 (GDB-2):** `DebugDemo.cpp`, a layer `engine:debug_demo` between the two, writes every frame through a `RenderFrameExchange` the application owns: a grid, a capsule walking a circle with its facing ray and melee arc, a sphere, boxes, a cylinder, a cone, an arc volume, two overlapping fills with opacity, labels, the world axes and a frame counter on top, and a camera orbiting the scene. The GodotLayer shows it, with the development overlay at the top-right (a checkbox per debug layer and what the frame drew). From slice 5 the demo is an extractor (`engine:debug_demo`) of the Presentation layer, drawn relative to the frame's origin like any other.
- **Slice 5 (SIM-2, CLI-1, GDB-3):** the stack is the input trace, a Simulation layer with a local `SimulationRuntime`, `MovingEntities.cpp` (`engine:moving_entities`), the Presentation layer and the GodotLayer. Sixteen walkers patrol squares on the plane around the slice 2 scene, steered only through a `CommandQueue`: a 20 Hz driver queues a Steer command when a walker reaches a corner, the Commands phase applies it, the Movement phase moves the walker. Four still crates stand at the corners. The Presentation layer draws them as debug shapes (`engine:debug_shapes`) and the presenter keeps them in Godot's RenderingServer by their IDs; the camera circles the plane once a minute. The CTest `godot.runic_client_moving_entities` checks 20 instances in Godot, the commands applied and the simulation's ticks.

- **Slice 7 (PHYS-1, PHYS-2, SIM-8, CLI-2):** `WalkScene.cpp` (`engine:walk_scene`) is the default scene; the slice 5 scene runs with `--moving-scene`. The stack is the input trace, the Simulation layer (physics enabled on its runtime), the Input layer, the scene, the Presentation layer and the GodotLayer. A capsule (a physics character) walks on a 64 m terrain (a Jolt mesh, flat in its middle, rolling hills around) 8,600 km from the world's origin, with a 0.3 m step it climbs, a 0.6 m ledge it does not, a wall and three crates it pushes. Keys: W A S D walk relative to the camera, Shift+W runs (its own action), Space jumps, R walks on its own (on and off), a left click logs a mark. The Input layer turns keys into actions, a command builder turns actions into walk commands, the Commands phase hands them to the character, and the camera follows the capsule. The terrain extractor (`engine:demo_terrain`) draws the terrain as shaded triangles with their wireframe and the controls above the capsule. The CTest `godot.runic_client_walk` presses R, Space and R through Godot and checks the capsule walked, jumped once and ends on the ground; `godot.runic_client_input` also checks that the click beside the test button becomes the mark action and the click on it does not.

- **Slice 8 (PHYS-3, GDB-4):** the walk scene registers a physics geometry extractor (`engine:demo_physics_geometry`): each frame it asks the simulation's physics for the bodies and the capsule within 100 m of the camera, into the frame's physics geometry, with the terrain as one cached debug mesh in the exchange's library. The GodotLayer's physics geometry layer draws it (toggles "Physics geometry" and "See what the server sees" in the development overlay). `--physics-check` adds an overlay layer that measures, every frame in Godot, the capsule's collision lines against its gameplay shape; the CTest `godot.runic_client_physics_view` expects one cached mesh uploaded once and the two lined up.

- **Slice 11 (WORLD-2):** `SpatialScene.cpp` (`engine:spatial_scene`, with `--spatial-scene`): 150 walkers drift, climb and dive in a 200 m box 6,400 km out, members of a World `SpatialIndex` whose membership the PostPhysics phase updates. Each realtime tick the Queries phase runs one query (a sphere, a box or an ellipsoid sweeping from a cave under the ground to an island in the sky) and checks it against testing every walker. A `VolumeSet` holds the cave (a turned tunnel and a chamber), a quest area, an indivisible island and a ship's deck circling the scene as a sensor. The extractor `engine:demo_spatial_view` draws the volumes, the occupied cells, the walkers and the query with what it found; the camera circles the scene. The CTest `godot.runic_client_spatial` runs it for 240 frames: every query matches testing every walker, and walkers pass through the cave.

A program is a composition root, not a module: no catalog row and no `Public/`. From APP-1 (slice 1) on it defines
`CreateApplication` for the Engine's entry point and stacks layers that modules supply. See
[Engine/Programs](../README.md).
